# Industry Classification & Probability Scoring

---

## 🟢 HIGH PROBABILITY (75-85% conversion if you reach them)

These industries have customers who research online BEFORE buying/booking. Being invisible online directly costs them revenue.

### Hair Salons & Barber Shops
- **Customer behavior:** Browse Instagram/Google before booking appointments
- **Decision drivers:** Portfolio (see past work), location + hours, reviews/trust
- **Website need:** CRITICAL (shows work, builds credibility, takes bookings)
- **Pain point:** "We get customers from word-of-mouth but lose the ones who Google us first"
- **Messaging:** "Show your work, build trust, book more clients"
- **Estimated close rate:** 40-60% of conversations
- **Priority:** START HERE

### Massage Therapists & Wellness Studios
- **Customer behavior:** Research therapist credentials + reviews before booking
- **Decision drivers:** Therapist bios, services offered, client testimonials, cleanliness cues
- **Website need:** CRITICAL (professionalism + reassurance)
- **Pain point:** "People want to know who they're trusting before they book"
- **Messaging:** "Build trust with new clients. Professional scheduling. Testimonials."
- **Estimated close rate:** 35-50% of conversations
- **Priority:** TIER 1 (high volume, high conversion)

### Fitness Studios & Gyms
- **Customer behavior:** Need to see class schedule + trainer profiles before committing
- **Decision drivers:** Class variety, trainer expertise, community, success stories
- **Website need:** CRITICAL (schedule must be discoverable, booking essential)
- **Pain point:** "Managing schedules across Instagram/Facebook is chaos. Inquiries get lost."
- **Messaging:** "Professional scheduling. Your classes discoverable. More members."
- **Estimated close rate:** 40-60% of conversations
- **Priority:** TIER 1 (high volume, high conversion)

### Restaurants, Cafes & Bars
- **Customer behavior:** Google before visiting (location, hours, menu, reviews)
- **Decision drivers:** Location + hours, menu, atmosphere, reviews
- **Website need:** CRITICAL (findability + credibility)
- **Pain point:** "People can't confirm our hours or see our menu before coming"
- **Messaging:** "Get found. Show your menu. Take reservations."
- **Estimated close rate:** 30-50% of conversations
- **Priority:** TIER 1 (high volume, high conversion)

### Beauty Services (Aesthetics, Nails, Skincare)
- **Customer behavior:** Browse photos + reviews, read service descriptions
- **Decision drivers:** Work quality (photos), service range, therapist bios, reviews
- **Website need:** CRITICAL (portfolio + booking)
- **Pain point:** "Instagram is our only storefront. We need a real online home."
- **Messaging:** "Showcase your artistry. Professional booking. Own your presence."
- **Estimated close rate:** 35-50% of conversations
- **Priority:** TIER 1 (high volume, high conversion)

## 🟡 MEDIUM PROBABILITY (30-50% conversion if targeted right)

These industries benefit from online presence, but the benefit is conditional. Score higher if they mention custom/specialty work or show growth ambition.

### Specialty Retail (Boutiques, Jewelry, Home Goods, Fashion)
- **Customer behavior:** Online discovery varies; depends on type
- **Decision drivers:** Product appeal, location, curation
- **Website need:** CONDITIONAL (helps if targeting beyond local foot traffic)
- **Scoring:** Score 7-8 if upscale/boutique; score 3-4 if commodity
- **Messaging:** "Show your curation. Reach beyond foot traffic."
- **Estimated close rate:** 20-35% of conversations
- **Filter:** Only approach if they show design sense + social presence
- **Priority:** TIER 2 (only after Tier 1 working)

### Custom Carpenters & Builders
- **Customer behavior:** Request portfolio of past work
- **Decision drivers:** Quality, style, reliability, past projects
- **Website need:** CONDITIONAL (portfolio can lead to higher-value contracts)
- **Scoring:** Score 7-8 if they mention custom/bespoke; score 2-3 if mass production
- **Messaging:** "Showcase your best work. Attract higher-value clients."
- **Estimated close rate:** 15-30% of conversations
- **Filter:** Only approach if they do custom work (not prefab/mass work)
- **Priority:** TIER 2 (secondary wave)

### Specialized Electricians (Smart Home, Solar, EV Charging)
- **Customer behavior:** Research credentials + certifications + portfolio for specialized work
- **Decision drivers:** Expertise proof, past projects, certifications
- **Website need:** CONDITIONAL (helps for specialized/high-ticket work)
- **Scoring:** Score 6-8 if specialized; score 2-3 if general commercial
- **Messaging:** "Build credibility for your expertise. Attract the right clients."
- **Estimated close rate:** 10-25% of conversations
- **Filter:** Only approach if they show specialization (smart home, solar, etc.)
- **Priority:** TIER 2 (secondary)

### Yoga Studios & Pilates
- **Customer behavior:** Browse class schedule + instructor bios before trying
- **Decision drivers:** Class times, instructor credentials, beginner-friendly, community
- **Website need:** MEDIUM (benefits from booking + schedule visibility)
- **Scoring:** 6-8 if growing; 4-5 if established with full schedule
- **Messaging:** "Professional scheduling. More students. Community hub."
- **Estimated close rate:** 30-45% of conversations
- **Priority:** TIER 1-2 (depends on growth signals)

### Personal Trainers & Coaching
- **Customer behavior:** Research coach credentials + testimonials before hiring
- **Decision drivers:** Certifications, results (testimonials/before-afters), specialization
- **Website need:** MEDIUM (portfolio of results helps)
- **Scoring:** 5-7 if freelance/building; 3-4 if established
- **Messaging:** "Show your results. Attract ideal clients."
- **Estimated close rate:** 20-35% of conversations
- **Priority:** TIER 2 (secondary wave)

### Plumbing (Young/Indie Plumbers Building Their Business)
- **Customer behavior:** Research reputation online before calling
- **Decision drivers:** Reviews, response time, pricing transparency
- **Website need:** CONDITIONAL (helps if building reputation)
- **Scoring:** 5-7 if young/new; 1-3 if established with full schedule
- **Messaging:** "Build your reputation online. Get better clients."
- **Estimated close rate:** 10-25% of conversations
- **Filter:** Only approach if they're young, new, or explicitly hiring
- **Priority:** TIER 2 (not primary)

## 🔴 LOW PROBABILITY (<15% conversion; skip initially)

These industries either don't need websites or are already oversaturated with referrals. Website won't materially change their business model.

### General Plumbers (Established, Busy)
- **Customer behavior:** Find via referrals or emergency searches (location-based)
- **Decision drivers:** Reputation, response time, price
- **Website need:** LOW (referrals handle demand; emergency calls are location-based)
- **Why no website:** Not laziness = they're already booked. Chose not to waste time.
- **Scoring:** 2-4 (too busy to care)
- **Messaging:** None worth trying (they'll ignore)
- **Estimated close rate:** 5-10% of conversations
- **Rule:** Skip unless they're explicitly new/young
- **Priority:** SKIP

### HVAC/Heating Installers (Established)
- **Customer behavior:** B2B relationships or emergency location-based searches
- **Decision drivers:** Reputation, availability, price
- **Website need:** LOW (work comes from builders/contractors, not consumers)
- **Why no website:** Work model doesn't require it
- **Scoring:** 1-3 (not your market)
- **Messaging:** None worth trying
- **Estimated close rate:** <5% of conversations
- **Priority:** SKIP

### Commodity Retail (Convenience Stores, Pharmacies, Basic Services)
- **Customer behavior:** Find by geography, not online research
- **Decision drivers:** Proximity, convenience
- **Website need:** LOW (website won't change "I need coffee nearby" decision)
- **Why no website:** Online presence doesn't drive their business model
- **Scoring:** 2-4 (foot traffic only)
- **Messaging:** None worth trying
- **Estimated close rate:** <10% of conversations
- **Priority:** SKIP

### Gas Stations, Car Washes, Laundromats
- **Customer behavior:** Proximity-based (Google Maps shows they exist)
- **Decision drivers:** Location, hours
- **Website need:** MINIMAL (Google Maps is enough)
- **Why no website:** Business model requires foot traffic; online presence doesn't help
- **Scoring:** 1-2
- **Priority:** SKIP

## SCORING FORMULA

Use this formula to score prospects consistently:

```
SCORE = (Industry_Weight × 0.5) + (Website_Status × 0.3) + (Business_Signals × 0.2)

Where:
- Industry_Weight: 1.0 (high) | 0.7 (medium) | 0.2 (low)
- Website_Status: 1.0 (none) | 0.7 (outdated) | 0.4 (good but old) | 0.1 (good + recent)
- Business_Signals: 1.0 (strong: recent reviews, active social, professional) | 0.5 (moderate) | 0.0 (none)

Examples:

Hair Salon + No Website + Strong Signals:
(1.0 × 0.5) + (1.0 × 0.3) + (1.0 × 0.2) = 1.0 → SCORE 10 (TIER 1)

Hair Salon + No Website + Weak Signals:
(1.0 × 0.5) + (1.0 × 0.3) + (0.0 × 0.2) = 0.8 → SCORE 8 (TIER 1)

Specialty Retail + Outdated Website + Strong Signals:
(0.7 × 0.5) + (0.7 × 0.3) + (1.0 × 0.2) = 0.76 → SCORE 7.6 (TIER 2)

Established Plumber + No Website + Some Signals:
(0.2 × 0.5) + (1.0 × 0.3) + (0.5 × 0.2) = 0.4 → SCORE 4 (TIER 3, SKIP)
```

**Rounding:** Round to nearest integer for final score.

## BUSINESS SIGNALS TO LOOK FOR

**Strong signals** (prospect is active + growing):
- ✅ Google Maps with recent reviews (last month)
- ✅ Instagram/Facebook with posts in last 2 weeks
- ✅ Ratings 4.5+ (consistent satisfaction)
- ✅ Professional storefront/appearance (photos)
- ✅ Clear hours + contact info listed
- ✅ Photos of work/products

**Moderate signals:**
- ⚠️ Google Maps with reviews 2-6 months old
- ⚠️ Social media active but infrequent (monthly)
- ⚠️ Rating 3.5-4.5
- ⚠️ Basic contact info available

**Weak signals:**
- ❌ No reviews anywhere
- ❌ Last social post 6+ months ago
- ❌ Rating < 3.5
- ❌ No clear contact info
- ❌ Unprofessional appearance
